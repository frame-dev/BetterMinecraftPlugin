Goal
----
Improve the Minecraft vanilla survival experience using only server-side
Spigot-compatible features.

No client mods are required.
No custom launcher is required.
No modded blocks, entities, dimensions, or biomes are required.
Optional resource-pack features should remain disabled by default.
